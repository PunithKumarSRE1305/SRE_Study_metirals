# Career goals — what "ready" means

## The sentence we are aiming at

After ~31 months, you should be able to sit in an SRE interview, point at a real repository, and say:

> I have operated Linux, networked services, automated AWS with Terraform, deployed on Kubernetes, watched the system with metrics/logs/traces, defined SLOs, taken incidents, written postmortems, and designed for failure. Here is the project. Here is a failure I caused on purpose. Here is what I changed.

Not:

> I know AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform.

## Target role profile

- Title: Site Reliability Engineer (or Platform/Production Engineer with SRE practices)
- Maturity: **Level 4**, reaching toward Level 5
- Evidence: assessments + the evolving portfolio, not certificates alone

## Outcome list (the real exam)

At the end you can:

- Operate Linux systems
- Understand networks
- Automate infrastructure
- Build cloud infrastructure
- Deploy applications
- Operate Kubernetes
- Build CI/CD pipelines
- Monitor systems
- Analyze logs
- Trace distributed applications
- Define SLIs / SLOs
- Design reliable systems
- Troubleshoot production incidents
- Perform capacity planning
- Analyze performance (your current strength, upgraded)
- Automate repetitive work
- Conduct incident response
- Write postmortems
- Design for failure
- Understand distributed systems
- Build highly available systems
- Run disaster-recovery exercises
- Conduct chaos experiments
- Reason about reliability trade-offs
- Communicate during incidents
- Explain technical concepts clearly
- Pass SRE interviews
- Demonstrate real SRE projects

## What we will not optimize for

- Collecting certifications as a substitute for labs
- Memorizing 400 Kubernetes flags
- Interview answers in year 1
- A public "I finished the roadmap" badge with 0% assessments

Interview prep starts in the **second half**, after you have scars from labs.
