# Linux weeks — free resources hub

Videos and courses are **supplements**. They do not complete a week.  
A concept is 🟢 only after the assessment in this repo.

## How to use media without wrecking the 2-hour day

```text
Your 120 minutes still own the lesson file.
Video: at most 20–30 minutes, paused, with a terminal open.
If a video and this repo disagree, this repo + your lab win.
Do not binge a 6-hour "Linux in one video" on a work night.
```

## Certifications — honest list

| Credential | Free? | When (this journey) | Note |
| ---------- | ----- | ------------------- | ---- |
| LFS101 course | Yes | Months 1–3, audit only | Do the labs here first |
| LFS101 verified certificate | Paid | Optional, after Month 3 | Not required |
| LFS162 course | Yes | After you have run a service (Month 6+) | SRE words, not Linux depth |
| Cisco **Networking Basics** (NetAcad) | Yes + digital badge | Month 3–4 | [netacad.com](https://www.netacad.com/courses/networking-basics?courseLang=en-US) |
| LFCA exam | **Paid** | Not before Month 12 | Course material is useful; do not buy the exam yet |
| CompTIA Linux+ / RHCSA | Paid | Year 2, optional | Knowledge first |

There is **no** free exam that makes you an SRE. There are free *courses*. We use those.
